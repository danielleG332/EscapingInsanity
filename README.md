# Escaping Insanity

**Studio:** Sunset Games  

## About the Game

*Escaping Insanity* is a 3D bullet-heaven RPG horror game where players control John Doe, a wheelchair-bound patient trying to escape an abandoned asylum overrun by zombies. Players must survive three blocked-off sections, defeat zombie bosses, and find the keys needed to escape while managing their health and sanity.

## Features

- **Combat:** Automatically fire projectiles at enemies.
- **Enemy AI:** Zombies chase and attack the player.
- **Health and Sanity:** Manage health and sanity to survive.
- **Leveling System:** Earn XP and choose upgrades to improve character stats or weapons.
- **Weapons:** Use ranged and close-range weapons.
- **Three Sections:** Explore dangerous areas of the abandoned asylum.
- **Boss Battles:** Defeat zombie bosses to progress toward escape.
- **Sanity Effects:** Changes in sanity affect the player's vision and combat abilities.

## Technology

- **Game Engine:** Unity
- **Programming Language:** C#
- **Environment:** 3D with a semi-top-down camera
- **Assets:** 2D projectile assets integrated into a 3D environment

## How to Play

1. Launch the game in Unity or run its built executable.
2. Control John Doe and navigate the abandoned asylum.
3. Avoid enemies and survive zombie attacks.
4. Defeat enemies to earn XP and unlock upgrades.
5. Collect healing and sanity-restoring items.
6. Defeat the zombie bosses and obtain the keys to escape.

## Development Goals

The primary goal is to create a playable single-player horror experience that combines bullet-heaven combat, RPG progression, and sanity-based mechanics.
